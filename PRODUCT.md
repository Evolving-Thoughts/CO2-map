# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The curious public: general visitors (often arriving from a shared link or the author's website) who explore casually, without climate expertise, and want to understand how their country and others compare.

## Product Purpose

An interactive world map (and globe) that resizes and colors countries by their CO₂ emissions relative to an equal per-capita share of the remaining carbon budget. Success means a visitor walks away with a visceral fairness insight: which countries overuse their fair share, and by how much.

## Positioning

Instead of showing absolute emissions, it measures every country against the same yardstick — an equal per-capita slice of the IPCC AR6 Paris budget — and makes the overuse physically visible by distorting country size.

## Operating Context

Static site on GitHub Pages (https://evolving-thoughts.github.io/CO2-map/). Visitors switch metrics, toggle country scaling, switch map/globe, and click countries for an info panel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Metrics: Paris 1.5°C, Paris 2.0°C, Territorial, Consumption-based, Historical cumulative.
- Country scaling toggle; info panel with stats, budget ratios, per-capita breakdowns.
- Paris and Consumption modes prefer consumption-based data, falling back to territorial.
- Budgets: 1.5°C ≈ 350 Gt (50%), 2.0°C ≈ 1,100 Gt (67%), spread per capita to 2050.
- Current stack: vanilla JS/HTML/CSS, Leaflet, TopoJSON, CartoDB Positron tiles, data generated by `generate_data.py`. (Not stated as a binding constraint.)
- **Must keep:** colorblind mode.

## Evidence on Hand

- Data: Our World in Data CO₂ dataset (Global Carbon Project 2024), UN 2023 population; `owid-co2-data.csv`, `js/co2data.js`.
- 215 countries with data, 241 territories mapped.
- No testimonials, usage stats, or press exist; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. One fair yardstick: every comparison is against an equal per-capita share.
2. Make the insight felt, not just read — the map itself should carry the argument.
3. Transparent methodology: sources and assumptions are always reachable.
4. Approachable for non-experts; detail is available on demand, not up front.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Colorblind mode is a required, permanent feature; color must never be the only carrier of meaning.
